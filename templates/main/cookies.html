Cookies, clearly explained

This policy lists every cookie category used on our website, what each one does, and how you stay in control. Short version: only strictly necessary cookies run by default — everything else waits for your consent.

1

What Are Cookies

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit our website. They help the Site remember your choices and understand how it is used — always within the limits you set in our consent banner.

This page details every category we use. For personal data in general, see our Privacy Policy.

Category Examples Default
Strictly necessary Consent choice, session, security Always on
Preferences Language, display options Off until accepted
Analytics Aggregated visit statistics Off until accepted
Marketing Personalized content and offers Off until accepted
2

Strictly Necessary

These cookies are essential for the Site to work and cannot be switched off. They are set in response to your actions, such as saving your consent choice or keeping a form secure.

  • Consent choice — remembers Accept, Reject or customized settings.
  • Security — protects forms against spam and abuse.
  • Load balancing — keeps browsing fast and stable.
3

Preferences

Preference cookies remember non-essential settings such as your language (FR / EN / AR) or display options, so you do not have to set them again on your next visit. They are off by default and start only if you enable Preferences via Customize in the banner.

4

Analytics

Analytics cookies produce aggregated statistics about visits and popular pages. They never identify you personally and are off by default. Enable Analytics via Customize if you accept them.

5

Marketing

Marketing cookies allow personalized content and offers. They are off by default and start only if you enable Marketing via Customize in the banner. You can withdraw consent at any time with immediate effect.

6

Managing Consent

You stay in control: use Accept All, Reject All or Customize in the cookie banner, change your choice later through your browser settings (delete or block cookies), or write to us.

Questions about cookies? Contact privacy@bsgroup.ml — we reply within 30 days.
