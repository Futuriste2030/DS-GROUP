Your privacy, our priority

This policy explains what data we collect when you use our website, why we collect it, and the rights you have over it. Short version: we collect the minimum, we never sell it, and you stay in control.

1

Data Controller

The data controller for personal data processed through this website is BS GROUP Construction LLC, 2464 Royal Ln. Mesa, New Jersey 45463, USA. As data controller, BS GROUP determines the purposes and means of processing your personal data described in this policy.

For any privacy-related question, you can reach our dedicated contact at privacy@bsgroup.ml. We commit to answering every privacy request within 30 days.

2

Data We Collect

We only collect data you actively provide, plus minimal technical data required for the Site to function. Here is the complete picture:

Data Source Purpose
Name, email, phone Contact & quote forms Replying to you, preparing quotes
Project details Quote form Accurate estimation
Resume & cover letter Job application form Recruitment process
Email address Newsletter forms Sending our newsletter
Cookie consent preferences Cookie banner (local storage) Remembering your choices

We do not collect sensitive data (health, religion, political opinions...) and we do not use fingerprinting or cross-site tracking technologies.

3

How We Use Your Data

Each use of your data relies on a specific legal basis. We process your personal data only for the following purposes:

  • Responding to your messages and preparing quotes — legal basis: steps prior to a contract, at your request.
  • Processing job applications — legal basis: steps prior to a potential employment contract.
  • Sending our newsletter — legal basis: your explicit consent, withdrawable at any time.
  • Remembering your cookie choices — legal basis: our legitimate interest in honoring your preferences.
  • Meeting legal obligations (accounting, construction warranties) — legal basis: legal requirement.

We do not use your data for automated decision-making or profiling, and we do not send marketing communications to anyone who hasn't consented.

4

Cookies & Tracking

On your first visit, our cookie banner lets you accept, reject or customize cookies by category. Your choice is stored locally in your browser and can be changed anytime via the cookie button at the bottom left of every page.

Category Examples Consent
Strictly Necessary Cookie consent storage Always on
Preferences Language selection (FR / EN / AR) Optional
Analytics Anonymous visit statistics Optional
Marketing Embedded map (Google Maps), video Optional
5

Data Sharing & Disclosure

We do not sell, rent or trade your personal data. We only share it in these limited circumstances:

  • Service providers bound by confidentiality (email delivery, hosting) — processing only on our instructions.
  • Legal authorities when required by law, court order or to protect vital interests.
  • Business transfer — in the event of a merger or acquisition, with prior notice and unchanged protections.
6

Data Retention

We keep your personal data only as long as necessary for the purpose it was collected, then delete or anonymize it:

Data type Retention
Quote requests (no contract signed) 24 months
Client project data Duration of contract + warranty period
Job applications (not hired) 12 months
Newsletter subscriptions Until you unsubscribe
Cookie preferences 6 months, then re-asked
7

Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction (including GDPR for EU residents), you have the following rights over your personal data. To exercise any of them, email privacy@bsgroup.ml — no fee, answer within 30 days.

Access

Get a copy of the data we hold about you.

Rectification

Correct inaccurate or incomplete data.

Erasure

Request deletion of your data ("right to be forgotten").

Portability

Receive your data in a machine-readable format.

Objection

Object to certain processing, including marketing.

Withdraw consent

Unsubscribe or change cookie choices anytime.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority if you believe your rights have not been respected.

8

Data Security

We apply industry-standard technical and organizational measures to protect your data: encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS) on every page, strict access controls limited to authorized personnel, regular software updates, and secure storage for form submissions and application documents.

No method of transmission over the Internet is 100% secure. While we strive to protect your data, we cannot guarantee absolute security. In the unlikely event of a data breach affecting you, we will notify you and the relevant authorities without undue delay, as required by law.

9

Children's Privacy

The Site is not directed at children under 16, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, contact us at privacy@bsgroup.ml and we will delete it promptly.

10

Changes & Contact

We may update this policy to reflect legal or operational changes. The version published on this page is the one in effect; significant changes will be highlighted on the Site. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page always reflects the latest revision.

Privacy questions?

Our data protection contact replies within 30 days — usually much faster.
